L'application

Ces données restent sur ton PC et ne sont jamais envoyées à l'éditeur : l'autorisation donnée par Bungie, tes réglages, tes conversations avec le Spectre, les données du jeu mises en mémoire et tes marques d'inventaire. Le micro ne sert que pour « parler au Spectre » : il écoute quand tu maintiens la touche choisie, ou quand tu dis le mot de réveil si tu l'as activé. Le son est traité sur ton PC et n'est pas envoyé ailleurs.

  • light.gg et D2Gunsmith : l'application demande leurs pages publiques d'une arme (seul le numéro de l'arme est envoyé).
  • Ollama et Hugging Face : si tu choisis une IA ou un modèle de reconnaissance vocale à télécharger.

Chacun de ces services voit ton adresse IP et applique sa propre politique.

Privacy

SpectreIA has no account to create, no server of its own, no analytics and no ads. Here is exactly what happens.

The application

This data stays on your PC and is never sent to the publisher: the permission granted by Bungie, your settings, your conversations with the Ghost, cached game data and your inventory marks. It lives in the application's data folder (%APPDATA%\spectre-ia). Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder erases everything.

The AI runs on your computer: your questions are not sent to any server.

The microphone is only used to "talk to your Ghost": it listens while you hold the chosen key, or when you say the wake word if you turned it on. The sound is processed on your PC and is not sent anywhere else.

The application's connections

  • Bungie (bungie.net): to read your account and game data, with your permission. You can withdraw it on bungie.net.
  • GitHub: to look for and download updates. GitHub sees your IP address.
  • light.gg and D2Gunsmith: the app requests their public page for a weapon (only the weapon's number is sent).
  • Ollama and Hugging Face: if you choose an AI or a speech recognition model to download.

Each of these services sees your IP address and applies its own policy.

The website

The website uses no cookies and no audience measurement. Your browser only keeps your theme and language choice, on your device. When the page loads, three services receive your IP address: Google Fonts (typefaces), jsDelivr (a 3D display library) and GitHub's API (to find the latest installer).

The contact form and email

If you write through the form, I receive your message, its type, the page language and your email address if you leave one. I only use it to answer you and improve SpectreIA. The message is stored at Cloudflare until I handle it, then I delete it, 12 months at the latest. Your IP address is not kept: only a scrambled fingerprint, erased after one hour, is used to limit how many messages can be sent. Nothing is sold or shared. If you email me directly, your mail provider and mine keep the message.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data I hold about you, or object to its use, by writing to the contact email. If you feel that isn't enough, you can contact the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.
